Undergraduate Faculties

Faculty Details

Faculty of Health Sciences

Dean: Dr. Mrs. A. O. Williams

CrestOak College of Health Sciences, Management, and Technology

Undergraduate programs are designed to meet standard national and clinical guidelines to foster first-class professional skills and competence.

Available Courses / Programs

Nursing Sciences (B.Sc.)
Medical Laboratory Science (BMLs)
Public Health
Physiology
Entry Requirements

Five O'Level Credit passes in WAEC/NECO/NABTEB including English Language, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, and Physics in not more than two sittings. Candidates with JAMB must satisfy the cut-off threshold of 140 (Nursing Sciences requires 200).

Standard Duration

4 - 5 Years (B.Sc. / BMLs / professional pathways)

Career & Job Outcomes

Licensed Nurse, Medical Lab Scientist, Public Health Administrator, Community Health Inspector.

Naira (₦) Fees & Funding

CCHSMT runs a student-focused affordable tuition structure. Invoices are generated in Naira (₦) and can be paid securely online via Paystack or bank transfer.
